# Unresolved questions

1. How much recovery is lost to the 250,000-trial cap, versus score ranking,
   detrending, data gaps, or astrophysical variability?
2. Does a full-resolution/coarse-to-fine search improve recovery without
   raising hard-negative triggers at a fixed host-level FAR?
3. How does the local search compare with current QLP/SPOC TCEs and TLS when
   all methods use the same inputs, holdout, and calibration protocol?
4. What is the false-alarm and classification behavior on known eclipsing
   binaries, blends, variable stars, and archived false alarms?
5. What is recovery as a function of period, duration, depth, star type, cadence,
   sector count, QLP extraction version, and baseline after accounting for
   target selection?
6. Are BJD/BTJD conversions, product flux selection, normalization, and
   quality masks lossless for every QLP/SPOC product family?
7. Can an independent person rebuild the exact frozen release and reproduce
   candidate CSVs from the recorded binary/source hash?
8. What local FPP conclusions remain stable when realistic Gaia neighbors,
   dilution, and high-resolution imaging constraints are varied?
9. Which misses would still be recovered if search warnings, candidate ranking,
   or the duration/period range changed? The current blind set is retired, so
   answer on a new holdout or preregistered injections.
